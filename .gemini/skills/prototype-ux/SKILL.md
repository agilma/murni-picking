# Prototype UX

Optimize Murni-Booth for operational staff.

Always prioritize:

- Fewest necessary interactions
- Clear next action
- Immediate feedback
- Visible progress
- Easy quantity editing
- Large touch targets
- Minimal navigation
- Low cognitive load

Every operational screen should answer:

1. Where am I?
2. What am I doing?
3. What do I do next?

Prefer one primary action per screen.

Do not add unnecessary confirmation dialogs.

Do not add navigation complexity.

Do not redesign approved UX without explicit instruction.
